**High findings exist (S1 to S8), so by `AGENTS.md` section 11 this gate is not passed.** The set is well organised, its numbers agree between the glossary, the stories and the NFRs (I found no numeric mismatch), the story, criterion and AI-story counts are correct (36, 171, 15), and it is honest about what is assumed and unmeasured. Its defects are almost all in the interactions between rules: session end against handover, the definition of a business-initiated message against consent and quiet hours, setup completeness against routing, retention against bookings, and the provider-failure path below the degraded-mode threshold. One central term, *slot*, has no definition at all.

Severity follows the reviewer instructions: High = would cause wrong behaviour, harm, rework or an untestable item; Medium = ambiguity or inconsistency; Low = wording.
