# Design review: the emergency phrase list and the unsupported-type reply (M3 unit U10, first part)

Date: 2026-11-28. Reviewed: `docs/design/notes/m3-u10a-emergency-unsupported.md`
as first committed, before any code. The reviewer was an independent
agent that did not write the note, working read-only in a copy of the
repository. The report is below as it was returned, unedited. The
author's response follows it.

## The report

Read: the note; parse.ts; sink.ts kind mapping; `plainText`/`folded`; `runTurn`; handover.ts; `beginTurn`/`joinHandover`/`abandonTurn`; sessions.ts; inbound.ts storing; the turn job handler; state-machines section 1; guardrail policy section 6; US-C12 and US-C6 8-10. Not checked: the U11 note's prose (I read its code only), `finishTurn`'s body, state-machines section 3 beyond one line, the lease length, the send path for a not-answering tenant, and the plan's U10 section (only fragments seen, so nothing on item 5); nothing was run.

**D1 High, section 2 E2 / "Not the model's to see".** An emergency in a session handed over 24 hours or more ago reaches the model and leaves the handover detached.
- Sequence: handover attached at T for `requested`; the customer keeps writing; at T+25h they write "he is not breathing".
- The trigger returns `added`, which does not move `attached_at` (handover.ts). `joinHandover` then finds the 24 hours over, detaches the now-urgent handover and returns "none".
- The turn goes on to the model, so an ordinary reply follows the emergency text. For a not-answering tenant the batch is abandoned unmarked and the trigger is applied again on the next batch.
- Change: do the 24-hour detach before the trigger, so the trigger re-attaches with a fresh time. Or end the turn in the trigger's own transaction (lease check, trigger, mark batch, release) and never pass through `joinHandover`. The note should also say which transaction the trigger runs in.

**D2 High, sections 2 and 5.** Matching misses that follow from the real `plainText`, none admitted.
- `plainText` keeps case. Lower-casing is `folded`, which is not exported and folds only Farsi yeh and keheh. The note's "lower case" needs `folded(plainText())`, exported.
- Curly apostrophe: "can’t breathe" (the iPhone default) does not match "can't breathe". Fold U+2019/U+02BC to `'` and list "cant breathe".
- Arabic: no folding of أ/إ/آ to ا, ى/ي, ة/ه. "لا استطيع التنفس" misses a phrase written with أ.
- The boundary rule blocks "ومش قادر اتنفس" and "النزيف". Allow the one-letter prefixes و ف ب ل and ال before a phrase.
- Joined spellings: Bengali "পারছিনা" against "পারছি না"; Urdu "آرہی" against "آ رہی", and ہ/ه; Hindi सांस/साँस; Tagalog "di makahinga", "'di", "hindi makahinga", the linker "-ng".
- "No letter" must mean `[\p{L}\p{M}]`, plus `\p{N}` before, as the existing `NOT_AFTER_WORD` has it. Otherwise a Devanagari or Bengali phrase matches inside a longer word before a vowel sign.
- Latin transliteration ("saans nahi aa rahi", "mesh 2ader atnaffas") is how much Hindi, Urdu and Arabic is typed. List it, or name it as a miss.
- A phrase split over two messages ("he's not" / "breathing") is missed by a per-text check. Also check the batch's texts joined by a space.
- Misspellings: admit them.

**D3 High, sections 1 and 2.** What a false alarm costs is understated.
- In M3 nobody is emailed and nobody can reply. "Is this an emergency number?", or "emergency appointment" to a clinic or emergency plumber, gets "contact emergency services" and then no answer to anything for up to 24 hours.
- A real emergency gets one text and the same silence. Then the 24-hour detach removes the urgent handover and the assistant resumes. None of this is said.
- Change: say it. Consider letting a phrase-only match (model flag not set) send the text, record the urgent reason and still answer, at least for packs where "emergency" is trade vocabulary, or allow a per-pack exclusion list.
- Every list checked against every text: short Tagalog or Roman words ("patay ang phone ko") will fire in other languages' chats. Require multi-word phrases or a minimum length.
- The note says "I'm dying…" is not on the list; the policy says it fires. One must change.

**D4 Medium, section 2 "Where".** Step 4a is per message at store time, "in every state"; the note moves it into the turn without saying so.
- Delay: 3 to 10 s of batching window, plus the whole of any running turn (busy, re-tried each second, bounded by model timeouts and the lease), plus job backlog. State the bound.
- Never checked: `beginTurn` returning `skipped: window-closed` (a late-delivered message with an old channel time) marks the batch with no turn. A session ended by `openSession` marks its unhandled messages likewise.
- Change: check in the store transaction, which holds the conversation lock, or list these paths as misses.

**D5 Medium, E2/E4.** `emergencyToldLately` counts any `pending` or `sending` telling, with no age limit, per conversation rather than per contact.
- Sequence: the text is pending for a tenant that cannot send; a second emergency is suppressed; the first then expires or fails. Nothing is sent until the customer writes again, when `joinHandover`'s `needsEmergency` re-tells.
- Change: say so; on a failed emergency telling, alert or re-queue. E3's "if the channel can send" needs the same sentence.

**D6 Medium, section 3 table.** Kinds against parse.ts and sink.ts.
- A document's caption is not read: `caption()` runs only for image and video. "Captions included" is wrong for a PDF captioned with an emergency.
- An unread template tap is stored as `quick-reply`. A channel type named `text`, `button` or `template` is stored as `unknown`.
- Meta's own type `unsupported` (polls, view-once and, I believe, deletes and edits) falls in "ignored": the customer said something and gets silence, against US-C12's "not ignored". Decide which.
- A voice note is `audio`: fine.
- Image plus a stop tap: no reply, count neither moved nor reset. Say it.

**D7 Medium, N2/N3/N6.**
- N3 "whatever it ends with" cannot hold for lost-lease, "newer-message", a thrown provider failure or `abandonTurn`: nothing is stored there. Say "when the batch is marked".
- N6 "nothing, as today" is false. Today the no-text check precedes `answering`, so the batch is marked. Say marked or left.
- N2 does not say what the count is afterwards. If it stays 2, then after the 24-hour detach every single image hands over again; if 0, two replies and one handover a day. The 0-to-100 range contradicts state-machines' 0-to-3.
- N5: say the count is not moved.

**D8 Low, for the second part.**
- Step 4a precedes step 5: the emergency text goes to an opted-out contact (policy: "before opt-out handling"). The note must say the second part keeps this.
- A STOP keyword or tap in a batch that E1 or step 10 marks as dealt with must still be applied. Today such a tap would be marked and lost.

**Could not fault**
- E6: a retry returns `added` and is told-lately, so no second text.
- E3's ordering: `joinHandover` marks the batch before the `answering` check, so no trigger loop (apart from D1).
- Image floods: two replies, then step 10 is silent.
- A captioned image or video stored as text, per US-C12 criterion 2.
- Counting at store time.
