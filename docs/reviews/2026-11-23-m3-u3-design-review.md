## The report

# Review: design note `docs/design/notes/m3-u3-profile.md` (M3 U3) and row 41

Reviewed in `/tmp/u3design`, branch `feat/302-m3-u3-profile`, commit `0a06d48`. Nothing was run but read-only git, grep, and one `node -e` for regex and arithmetic checks. "Note:N" is a line of the design note.

## Verdict

**Build after changes.** The shape is sound: three stores, one assembling function with no contact input, an operator's loader, a derived answering test. It is not ready as written. Five things would be baked into migration 0007 or the role grants and are wrong or undecided: (1) the cross-tenant role, whose credential the internet-facing API and the worker both hold, gains write access to every tenant's prices, policies and support contact, with no threat-model change and no decisions row; (2) "no character that cannot be printed", if it is the rule `ops` already uses, refuses ZWNJ, ZWJ, bidi marks and line breaks, which correct Urdu, Persian, Hindi, Bengali and mixed Arabic/Latin text need; (3) the support contact as free text contradicts the glossary and the O5 check, and leaving it out of the profile is a decision about customers with no row; (4) currency without a list leaves the minor-unit exponent unknown, so a price in minor units cannot be rendered or checked (KWD, BHD and OMR have three decimals); (5) the note says `tenant list` is unrecorded, and it is recorded. Beyond those: field limits and the hours shape contradict the API design without being listed as departures, the character budget is not comparable across the product's languages, and row 41 understates what a "no" costs.
