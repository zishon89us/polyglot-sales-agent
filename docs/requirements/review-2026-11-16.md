# Requirements review, 2026-11-16

Issue #46 · Reviewed: `AGENTS.md` (scope), `vision.md`, `personas.md`,
`user-stories.md`, `nfr.md`, `risks.md`, `traceability.md`

The maintainer asked for the requirements to be checked hard before design
starts. This review cross-checked every document against the others, asking
of each story: can it be built and tested as written, and does anything it
depends on exist?

**Result: 12 findings, 10 of them high severity. 11 are resolved in this pull
request; 1 depends on external verification. 5 questions need the maintainer.**

The first version of the requirements had real gaps. The largest: nothing
said how a business gets set up, how staff learn of a handover, or how times
and time zones work for bookings, and two headline metrics could not have
been computed.

A review like this lowers the chance of a requirements defect surfacing
later; it does not remove it. This review was done by the same agent that
wrote the documents, which is a known weakness. The maintainer's own read,
and a pilot, are the independent checks.

## Findings

Severity: **High** would have caused wrong behaviour, rework during build, or
an untestable story. **Medium** would have caused ambiguity. **Low** is
wording.

| ID | Severity | Finding | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | High | No story covered a customer sending several short messages in a row, the normal way people chat. As written, each fragment would get its own reply. | Added US-C11 (#47), NFR-P5 and NFR-R7. |
| F2 | High | Voice notes and images are out of scope, but nothing said what happens when a customer sends one. | Added US-C12 (#48). |
| F3 | High | `AGENTS.md` requires recorded consent before contacting a customer, but no story did. Reminders and order confirmations are exactly the messages that need it. | Added US-C13 (#49). |
| F4 | High | Handover (US-C6) marked a conversation and told the customer a person would reply, but nothing told the person. | Added US-C14 (#50). |
| F5 | High | Booking stories did not mention time zones, opening hours, service duration or past times. | Added US-B5 (#51); time zone is a required tenant setting. |
| F6 | High | No story created a tenant or connected a number. Every other story assumed one existed. | Added US-D6 (#52). |
| F7 | Medium | `AGENTS.md` names owner and agent roles; no story defined what an agent may not do. | Added US-D7 (#53). |
| F8 | High | No-show rate is a headline metric for the booking pack, but nothing recorded whether a customer attended. | Added US-D8 (#54). |
| F9 | High | Staff could not block time or add phone and walk-in bookings, so the assistant's "real availability" would have been wrong from the first day. | Added US-D9 (#55). |

## Questions for the maintainer

These change scope or need a decision the agent may not make.

| ID | Question | Options | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Refused-delivery rate is the cash-on-delivery pack's outcome metric, but the only store integration in scope is `order.created`, so the product never learns whether a parcel was delivered. | (a) Add a second signed store event reporting the delivery outcome. (b) Remove the metric from the product and measure it by hand in a pilot. | (a). It is one more webhook of the same kind, and without it the pack cannot show its own value. |
| Q2 | For business-initiated messages, which languages must be supported at launch? Each may need its own approved template. | (a) Tenant default language only. (b) A fixed short list per tenant. | Decide after #37 reports the current template rules. |
| Q3 | Should the store's order webhook carry the customer's language or locale, so the first confirmation can be sent in it? | (a) Optional `locale` field, falling back to the tenant default. (b) Always the tenant default. | (a). |
| Q4 | How are staff notified of a handover (US-C14)? | (a) Dashboard list plus email. (b) Also a WhatsApp message to a staff number, which is a business-initiated message with its own cost and template. | (a) for the MVP. |
| Q5 | The glossary proposes quiet hours: no business-initiated messages between 22:00 and 08:00 tenant time. This delays overnight order confirmations until morning. | (a) Keep as the default, configurable per tenant. (b) No quiet hours. | (a). Flagged because it is a new rule this review introduced, not something previously agreed. |

## Checked and found consistent

- Every assumption in `vision.md` (A1–A6) has a matching risk in `risks.md`.
- Every "target in `nfr.md`" referenced by a story now has a row in `nfr.md`.
- Every non-goal in `AGENTS.md` is repeated in `vision.md` and contradicted by
  no story.
- Every persona has at least one story, and every story names a persona.
- Every AI-marked story has a corresponding dimension planned for the eval
  plan (#43); the mapping itself is written in M1.
- Every story has an issue, a milestone and a traceability row.

## Not checked

- Whether the stated channel behaviour (reply window, templates, button
  replies, message types) matches the current official documentation. That is
  #37, and US-C9, US-C12 and F12 depend on it.
- Whether real businesses in the target niches work the way the personas
  assume. Only interviews or a pilot can check that.
- Legal sufficiency of the consent, retention and deletion requirements.

## Effect on scope

The MVP grew from 23 to 32 stories. None of the nine additions adds a new
channel, pack or integration; US-D8 and US-D9 add two small dashboard
functions. Milestone M5 now carries the most work and may need splitting; that
is decided when M4 ends.
